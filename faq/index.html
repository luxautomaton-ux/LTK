What is included in a Website Studio kit?

A Chat-Kit.md build brief, a companion handbook, starter source, and supporting workflow resources. Check each kit’s detail page for its specific contents and prerequisites.

Is a kit a finished website?

A kit is a foundation you customize. You supply your content and business details, review the code and design, and connect any services your project needs.

How do I use a Chat Kit?

Attach Chat-Kit.md to your preferred AI chat, describe your project, and follow the included instructions. A coding agent can work with the starter files. Review generated work before publishing.

Do I need to know how to code?

You can explore prompts and planners without coding. Building and deploying a starter project may require development tools and technical help. Requirements vary by kit.

How do I open the free sample?

Download and extract the ZIP, then open README.md. Text and Markdown files open in a text editor; CSV planners open in a spreadsheet application. No credit card is required.

Can I buy a membership today?

Paid checkout is not available in this preview. The membership page describes the planned offer. The local demo vault can be explored without a paid subscription.

Does the membership include AI subscriptions or hosting?

External AI tools, hosting, domains, and other third-party services are separate. Their availability, charges, and terms are set by those providers.

Can I use the kits for client work?

Check the included license and third-party notices for each kit. The Licensing page explains the review process. A paid commercial-use license must be confirmed before checkout opens.

How do cancellation and refunds work?

See Refunds & Cancellation for the current launch status. No paid subscription is created here, and this preview cannot process a cancellation or refund.

Can I change the appearance or stop animation?

Use the header’s Light / Dark button to change the palette. Your choice is remembered on this browser. Use Pause motion to stop decorative animation; your device’s reduced-motion preference is also respected.

Where do I get help?

Start with the Support and Contact pages. Support sending is not connected yet, so no request is received through this preview.
