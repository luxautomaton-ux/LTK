How Build Credits work

Build Credits are a member benefit used to unlock eligible toolkits. Credits are issued monthly, can roll for up to 90 days while the membership remains active, and are not cash or refundable stored value. Credits are an access mechanic—not a cryptocurrency, gift card, or cash balance.

WHY IT IS NOT A SIMPLE PROMPT KIT

Each premium toolkit is a system an AI can actually work from.

The value is in connecting the idea to the build: context, instructions, starter material, workflow, and review standards all travel together.

01

Chat Kit

An AI-readable project brief that carries the build direction into a supported AI chat or coding workspace.

02

Prompts

Reusable prompts designed around the specific outcome rather than generic prompt lists.

03

04

Original project files and examples that give the build a real starting point.

05

A practical reference that explains the approach, tools, decisions, and adaptation path.

06

Workflow

A defined sequence from idea to build, review, and handoff.

07

08

WHY THE PRICING IS STRUCTURED THIS WAY

Pay for momentum, not a giant ZIP dump.

Unlimited access at a bargain price would reward one-time downloading and make it harder to fund updates, better documentation, new kits, support, and integrations. The club is designed around continued building.
