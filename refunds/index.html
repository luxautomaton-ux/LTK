No paid checkout in this preview

This preview cannot create or charge a subscription. There is no active preview purchase to cancel or refund. Free sample downloads have no purchase price.

Before membership opens

The business must confirm refund eligibility, the request window, handling of downloaded digital goods, billing-cycle access, and an operational cancellation channel. These choices have not yet been supplied. No blanket no-refund rule or invented refund window is imposed here.

A clear purchase decision

Before payment, the purchase screen should show the total price, billing interval, recurring-charge terms, included access, cancellation steps, and the adopted refund policy. Any rights provided by applicable law must remain available.

Billing help

Once billing is active, use the verified contact channel or account billing portal described at purchase. This preview cannot send a billing request or make an account change. Do not submit full card details in support messages.
