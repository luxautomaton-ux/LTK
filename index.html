What is a Chat Kit?+

A single Markdown file you attach to your AI chat. Each Website Studio file includes the build brief, prompts, four embedded skills, workflow, and starter source.

Do I need to be a developer?+

You can use the handbook and prompts to plan your site without coding. Building and running the starter requires a coding agent or a local development setup.

Is this a finished website?+

Each kit is a complete building resource. Your business details, final design, testing, and publishing are part of your own project.

Can I try something before joining?+

Yes. The Business Starter Sample is free to download with no account or credit card. The premium Website Studio kits are previewed here; membership checkout is coming soon.
