Getting started

Let’s make it simple.

Which website kit should I choose?

Choose the experience you want to create. Motion kits suit scroll stories; 3D & Glass kits focus on a signature scene; Commerce kits focus on products; Brand & SaaS kits help present your work or software.

How do I open the free sample?

Download the ZIP from the Free Sample page, extract it on your device, and begin with README.md. The CSV planners open in a spreadsheet app, and the text and Markdown files open in a text editor.

How do I use a Chat Kit?

Attach the kit’s Chat-Kit.md file to your AI chat and add your business details. A coding agent can work with the starter source. You review the result and approve any external tools or publishing.
